# Awesome-AI-Compliance-Platform

## Top AI Compliance Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on AI Governance, Model Risk Management, EU AI Act Compliance, Responsible AI Policy & Audit-Ready AI Inventories*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **AI Compliance**. These systems help organizations inventory AI systems, classify risk, document controls, align with frameworks (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001), and produce audit-ready evidence for regulators and internal governance.



**Examples** include Credo AI, Holistic AI, FairNow, Trustible, Monitaur, ModelOp, IBM watsonx.governance, SAS AI Governance, OneTrust AI Governance, Microsoft Purview AI Hub, Credal, and CalypsoAI (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise AI-GRC suites are largely commercial. Open tools for **risk classification**, **documentation**, **guardrail evidence**, and **community AI-GRC platforms** (e.g. AegisAI, AiExponent-style tooling) are growing. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Credo AI](https://www.credo.ai/)**  

  AI governance platform for policy, risk assessment, and continuous compliance workflows across the AI lifecycle.



- **[Holistic AI, FairNow, Trustible](https://www.holisticai.com/)**  

  Platforms focused on AI risk, fairness, and governance documentation for regulated and enterprise deployments.



- **[Monitaur, ModelOp](https://monitaur.ai/)**  

  Model risk management and AI operations governance tools for inventory, monitoring, and control of production models.



- **[IBM watsonx.governance, SAS AI Governance, Microsoft Purview AI Hub, OneTrust AI Governance](https://www.ibm.com/watsonx/governance)**  

  Enterprise governance modules within major software stacks—policy, lineage, risk, and compliance for AI systems.



- **[Credal, CalypsoAI](https://www.credal.ai/)**  

  Platforms bridging secure AI usage, policy enforcement, and governance for enterprise LLM and agent deployments.



- **[Other commercial AI compliance & governance platforms](https://www.credo.ai/)**  

  Additional solutions for AI inventory, impact assessment, and regulatory reporting.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[AegisAI](https://github.com/SdSarthak/AegisAI)**  

  Open-source AI-GRC platform oriented to EU AI Act workflows—system registration, risk classification, technical documentation, plus LLM guard and regulatory RAG modules.



- **[AiExponent open tools (LitmusAI, RiskForge, etc.)](https://github.com/aiexponenthq)**  

  Open-source utilities mapped to AI Act obligations—prohibited-practice screening, risk management file generation, and related compliance artefacts (Apache 2.0).



- **[RiskReady Community / open AI-native GRC](https://riskready.eu/)**  

  Open-source, self-hosted GRC-style stacks exploring AI-assisted risk, controls, and compliance workflows with human-in-the-loop approvals.



- **[Model cards & documentation templates](https://github.com/huggingface/model-card-toolkit)**  

  Open toolkits and standards for documenting models (intended use, metrics, risks)—foundational for compliance evidence packs.



- **[NIST AI RMF & open playbooks](https://github.com/search?q=NIST+AI+RMF+OR+AI+risk+management+open+source)**  

  Community implementations and checklists aligned to NIST AI Risk Management Framework and similar voluntary standards.



- **[NeMo Guardrails, LLM Guard & evidence of controls](https://github.com/NVIDIA/NeMo-Guardrails)**  

  Open guardrail frameworks whose configuration and logs can serve as technical control evidence in governance programs.



- **[AI inventory & registry open prototypes](https://github.com/search?q=AI+system+inventory+OR+model+registry+governance)**  

  Lightweight open registries for tracking AI systems, owners, and risk tiers inside an organization.



- **[Audit & logging open stacks](https://github.com/search?q=AI+audit+log+OR+model+decision+logging)**  

  Tools for immutable decision and prompt logging used in compliance and incident review.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **EU AI Act–oriented tooling**: AegisAI and AiExponent-style screeners/risk-file generators.

- **Documentation**: Model cards and system cards as baseline compliance artefacts.

- **Control evidence**: Open guardrails + logging as demonstrable technical measures.

- **Composable stacks**: Inventory sheet/registry + risk classification + model cards + guardrail configs + audit logs.

- Commercial platforms still lead in multi-framework policy engines, workflow, and board-level reporting.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Open **risk classification and documentation tools** plus **model cards** and **guardrail evidence** form a minimal compliance pack.  

**AegisAI**-class projects aim at fuller AI-GRC workflows.  

Commercial platforms (Credo AI, Holistic AI, IBM watsonx.governance, OneTrust, Microsoft Purview AI Hub, ModelOp, etc.) deliver enterprise process, integrations, and scale.  

Many organizations combine commercial governance platforms with open technical controls (guardrails, evals, logging). Fully open compliance programs are feasible for smaller estates with strong legal/process ownership; regulated enterprises typically need commercial GRC depth.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- AI compliance is jurisdiction-specific and evolving (EU AI Act, sector rules, etc.). Software does not replace legal advice, qualified risk assessment, or required conformity procedures. High-risk systems may need notified bodies or formal audits.

- Open-source tools help structure documentation and controls but do not guarantee regulatory acceptance. Commercial platforms shift process and support burden to the vendor. Always involve legal and compliance stakeholders.
